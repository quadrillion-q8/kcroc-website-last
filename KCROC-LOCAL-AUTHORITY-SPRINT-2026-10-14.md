# KCROC Local Authority Sprint — Kuwait

**Prepared:** 14 October 2026  
**Goal:** Improve qualified Kuwait-local enquiries for existing commercial pages without creating thin location URLs or implying that KCROC operates branches where it does not.

1. Complete the production build after the latest graph and generated SEO projection are synchronized.
2. Check these pages on the live domain after deployment:

3. In Search Console, keep the Country filter set to **Kuwait** and compare the same date windows before/after the changes. Separate query and page tables; query anonymization and row limits mean the two exports may not sum to the country total.
4. Confirm that Farwaniya is described as a pickup/service area, not a walk-in branch. Hawalli is the physical workshop location.

## 2. Google Business Profile checklist

- Verify the primary category and choose only secondary categories that accurately describe services actually provided.
- Ensure the public business name, phone, Hawalli address/pin, hours, website and service information match the real profile and current business operations.
- Add current, original photographs of the shop entrance/signage, repair benches, diagnostic equipment and team at work. Avoid generic stock photos and do not imply other branch locations.
- Use a tagged website URL if practical so visits from the Business Profile can be differentiated in analytics.
- Review the profile's available performance report monthly for calls, website clicks, direction requests and other supported interactions.
- Respond professionally to reviews without publishing customers' private repair details.
- Keep one eligible profile for the real workshop; do not create profiles for each pickup/service area.

## 3. Review request process

Ask customers neutrally after a completed repair. Do not buy reviews, offer incentives, filter requests to happy customers only, or prescribe the rating/wording. Use the actual Google review link from the Business Profile.

### English template

> Thanks for choosing KCROC. If you would like to share your experience, you can leave an honest Google review here: [Google review link]. We appreciate feedback either way—there is no incentive and no preferred wording.

### Arabic template

> يعطيك العافية، وشكرًا لاختيارك KCROC. إذا حاب تشارك تجربتك، تقدر تكتب تقييمك الصادق على Google من هذا الرابط: [رابط التقييم]. نقدّر رأيك بكل الأحوال، وما في أي مقابل للتقييم أو صياغة مطلوبة.

Keep a simple monthly log of completed jobs, review requests sent, new reviews and owner responses. Do not record unnecessary customer details in the log.

## 4. Local citations and business details

1. Create a canonical business-information sheet copied from the live Business Profile: business name, telephone, street address, map pin, hours, website and description.
2. Audit real directory/business listings that already mention KCROC and correct outdated details. Prioritize relevant Kuwaiti business directories and industry listings over volume.
3. Claim or update legitimate supplier/partner listings when they genuinely exist.
4. Do not submit KCROC to spam directories, create fake locations, or pay for bulk exact-match links.

## 5. Relevant local links and mentions

Build relationships, not a bulk backlink campaign. Suitable avenues may include legitimate Kuwait technology communities, repair/parts suppliers, business organizations, educational/office IT partners and local publishers where there is a real relationship or useful contribution.

Offer something useful: a practical laptop-care guide for Kuwait's climate, a safe troubleshooting explainer, or a genuinely documented repair-method article. Links should be editorially earned and use natural anchors. Do not require exact-match anchors such as “computer repair Kuwait.”

**Case-study publication gate:** the current source has three case-study records with `customerConsent.granted: false` and empty evidence arrays. Do not promote these as verified testimonials or use them for outreach until permission and supporting evidence are documented. Verify whether the narratives, prices, comparison quotes, photographs and customer contexts are publishable. If permission/evidence cannot be obtained, remove or rewrite the specific claims and avoid identifying context before continuing promotion.

## 7. Lead and revenue tracking

Track these separately rather than counting every click as a lead:

1. Contact initiated: phone click, WhatsApp click or booking submission.
2. Qualified enquiry: actual repair need and a serviceable location.
3. Quote/booking: pickup arranged and quote issued.
4. Completed repair: approved job, completed work and revenue in KWD.

Attribute the initial source/landing page when feasible. Review outcomes by service and area each month. Use Search Console for organic visibility and analytics/job records for business outcomes; do not infer booked jobs from search clicks alone.

## 8. Monthly review

- Compare matching 28-day and 56-day Kuwait-only windows.
- Review query-to-page alignment, clicks, CTR and positions for mobile and desktop separately.
- Review Business Profile interactions and new/recent reviews.
- Review qualified leads, approved quotes, completed jobs and revenue.
- Make one measured page/snippet change at a time where possible, and avoid declaring a win until there is enough post-crawl data.
