Frequently Asked Questions

How long does chip-level motherboard repair take?

Most component-level diagnostics and micro-soldering repairs are completed within 24–48 hours, depending on the severity of the logic board damage and component availability.

Is it cheaper to repair a motherboard or replace it?

Component-level repair is significantly more cost-effective. Replacing an entire motherboard often costs 60-80% of the device's value, whereas micro-soldering fixes the specific failed component for a fraction of that price.
