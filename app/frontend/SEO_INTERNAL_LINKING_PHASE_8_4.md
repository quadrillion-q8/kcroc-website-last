Preserve international editorial copy and intentional language alternates. Prefer relevant Arabic troubleshooting pages where a real Arabic twin exists. Reinforce /ar (technician), /ar/computer-repair-kuwait (computer repair), and /ar/laptop-repair-kuwait (laptop repair), without keyword stuffing. Nearby anchors retain proximity intent. Arabic laptop, charging port, screen, hinge and keyboard service pages surface relevant diagnostic paths; Arabic Hawalli distinguishes the physical lab and national homepage. Arabic problem pages respect graph service priority and use locale-aware guide language labels.

The rendered link audit checks every sitemap page's main-content anchors against canonical sitemap paths, counts unique inbound source pages, and records non-sitemap destinations and pages with no contextual inbound edges. Header/footer navigation and client-only states are not counted. Reporting alone does not prove an orphan or broken URL. Required new localized edges are strict build gates; unrelated legacy candidates are report-only pending investigation. No automatic link injection into global articles, no new URLs or redirects, no pricing, image or CSS changes.
