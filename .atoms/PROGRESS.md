# Requirements & Progress

## Requirements Overview
Build an online computer repair service booking platform (KCROC) with service browsing, booking form, gallery, service-specific landing pages, and WhatsApp-based confirmations.

## User Stories
- As a customer, I can browse available repair services and pricing.
- As a customer, I can book a repair by selecting device type, describing the issue, and scheduling a pickup.
- As a customer, I can receive booking confirmations via WhatsApp.
- As a visitor, I can view dedicated landing pages for laptop repair, MacBook repair, battery/screen replacement, and gaming PC cooling.
- As an admin, I can manage service bookings via authenticated access.
